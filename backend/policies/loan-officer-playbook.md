# Loan Officer Playbook

Internal guidance for loan officers and credit analysts. Do not share with customers.

## Handling Exceptions

Exceptions to product rules (for example a credit score slightly below the minimum, or FOIR between 50% and 55%) need written justification and approval from a credit analyst. Never process an exception without recording the reason in the application notes.

## Escalation Rules

Escalate all loans above ₹10 lakh to a senior credit analyst. Escalate any case with suspected document tampering or identity mismatch to the compliance team immediately.

## Customer Communication

Never promise approval or a specific interest rate before the credit decision. Use phrases like "indicative" and "subject to credit assessment". Do not ask for or share full PAN or Aadhaar numbers over chat or email.

## Requesting Documents

When documents are missing, request them through the application so the customer sees the request in their status timeline. Name the exact document needed (for example "Address proof: Aadhaar or recent utility bill") and keep the request polite and specific.
