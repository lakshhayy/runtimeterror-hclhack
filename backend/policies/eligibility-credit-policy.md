# Eligibility and Credit Policy

## Minimum Credit Scores

The minimum credit bureau score required by product type:

- Home loans: 700
- Personal loans: 680 (Salary Plus Loan: 700)
- Vehicle loans: 650
- Education loans: 650, and a co-applicant (parent or guardian) with a regular income is required

Applications below the minimum score are not approved automatically and may be declined after review.

## Repayment Capacity (FOIR)

FOIR (Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio) is the share of net monthly income that goes towards all EMIs, including the new loan. The Kestrel Bank FOIR limit is 50%. Applicants at or below 40% are generally considered comfortably eligible.

## Minimum Income

Minimum net monthly income is ₹25,000 for salaried customers and an average of ₹35,000 per month (from ITR) for self-employed customers. Students apply through a co-applicant who meets the income requirement.

## Fair Lending

Kestrel Bank does not consider gender, religion, caste, marital status or any other protected attribute in credit decisions. Decisions are based only on repayment capacity, credit history, verified documents and product rules. Every automated assessment is reviewed by a human credit analyst before a final decision.
