# Vehicle Loan Terms and Conditions

These terms apply to the Kestrel New Car Loan (VL-201) and the Kestrel Pre-Owned Car Loan (VL-204). This version replaces version 2026.1 from 1 July 2026.

## Eligibility

Applicants must be Indian residents of legal age with a regular source of income. Salaried and self-employed customers are both eligible. A minimum credit score of 650 is required. Total monthly EMIs, including the new vehicle loan, should not exceed 50% of net monthly income. Pre-owned cars must be no older than 8 years at the end of the loan tenure.

## Interest Rates

New car loans are offered from 9.0% to 11.5% per annum and pre-owned car loans from 11.0% to 14.0% per annum, on a fixed-rate basis. The final rate depends on the credit assessment, the loan amount and the tenure. The rate stays fixed for the full tenure.

## Foreclosure and Part-Prepayment

Foreclosure is allowed after 6 EMIs have been paid, with a 2% charge on the outstanding principal. Part-prepayment of up to 25% of the outstanding principal per financial year is free of charge after 6 EMIs. Prepayments above 25% in a financial year carry a 2% charge on the excess amount.

## Late Payment

If an EMI is not paid by its due date, a late payment charge applies as listed in the Fees and Charges Schedule. Repeated missed EMIs are reported to credit bureaus and may lead to repossession of the vehicle as per the loan agreement.
