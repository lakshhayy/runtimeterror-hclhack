# Fees and Charges Schedule

## Processing Fees

Processing fees are charged once on the sanctioned loan amount and are deducted at disbursal.

- Personal loans: 1.5% of the loan amount plus 18% GST (Salary Plus Loan: 1.0% plus 18% GST).
- Home loans: 0.5% of the loan amount (0.75% for the Self-Employed Home Loan).
- Vehicle loans: 1.0% for new cars and 1.5% for pre-owned cars.
- Education loans: nil for Education Loan India; 1.0% for the Global Education Loan.

## Payment Bounce Charges

If an EMI payment (NACH mandate, ECS or cheque) bounces, a charge of ₹500 per bounce applies, plus applicable GST.

## Late Payment Charges

Overdue EMIs attract a late payment charge of 2% per month on the overdue amount, calculated from the due date until the date of payment.

## Other Charges

Duplicate statement: ₹200. Loan account statement by email: free. Change of repayment mode: ₹500. No-objection certificate after loan closure: free.
