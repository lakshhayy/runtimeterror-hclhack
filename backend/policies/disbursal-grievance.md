# Disbursal and Grievance Redressal

## Disbursal Timelines

After a loan is sanctioned and the agreement is signed:

- Personal and vehicle loans are disbursed within 2 working days.
- Home loans are disbursed within 7 working days, after property verification.
- Education loans are disbursed directly to the institution as per the fee schedule.

## How to Raise a Grievance

Step 1: Contact your loan officer or call customer care. Most issues are resolved within 7 working days.

Step 2: If unresolved, write to the Grievance Redressal Officer. A response is given within 15 working days.

Step 3: If you are still not satisfied after 30 days, you may approach the RBI Ombudsman under the Integrated Ombudsman Scheme.

## Status Updates

Customers can track their application status, next steps and any requested documents in the LoanLens app at any time.
