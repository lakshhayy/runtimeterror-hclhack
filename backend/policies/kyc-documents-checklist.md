# KYC and Document Checklist

## Salaried Applicants

- PAN card
- Aadhaar card (identity and address proof)
- Salary slips for the last 3 months
- Bank statement for the last 6 months showing salary credits

## Self-Employed Applicants

- PAN card
- Aadhaar card (identity and address proof)
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) for the last 2 years, with computation of income
- Bank statement for the last 12 months
- GST registration certificate or other business proof (shop and establishment licence, Udyam certificate)

## Students (Education Loans)

- Admission letter from the institution
- Fee structure for the course
- PAN and Aadhaar of the student
- Income proof of the co-applicant (salary slips or ITR, as applicable)

## Document Format

Documents can be uploaded as PDF, JPG or PNG files of up to 5 MB each. All pages must be clear and readable. Address proof is mandatory; Aadhaar, a recent utility bill or a registered rent agreement are accepted.
